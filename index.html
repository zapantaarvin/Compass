10 Money Rules for a Dream Life

The principles your financial plan will be built on

RULE 01

Know your dream before building your budget

A plan without purpose becomes a restriction you'll quit. Money is a tool — define what it's for first.

RULE 02

Track by categories, not every purchase

Obsessing over $4 coffee misses the point. The goal is intentionality across your whole spending pattern.

RULE 03

Keep fixed costs ≤ 55–60% of take-home pay

High fixed costs are the #1 source of money stress. The lower, the more flexibility and freedom you have.

RULE 04

Invest for future-you before expanding lifestyle

The 401(k) match is free money. Get it first. Then invest at least 10% of take-home before spending on wants.

RULE 05

Save 5–10% for emergencies and near-term goals

Life will throw curveballs. Room for error isn't pessimism — it's the most important financial plan of all.

RULE 06

Guilt-free spending is not optional, it's required

A joyless budget is one you'll abandon. Spend extravagantly on what you love. Cut ruthlessly on what you don't.

RULE 07

Build room for error

Survival beats optimization every time. The ability to endure a bad month is worth more than a perfect plan that breaks.

RULE 08

Live below your means — wealth is what's unseen

The car you drive is wealth spent. The brokerage account no one sees is wealth built. Saving rate drives independence.

RULE 09

Avoid comparison spending

"Never enough" and status chasing destroy satisfaction. Enough is a skill that most people never develop.

RULE 10

Make decisions you can actually stick with

A reasonable plan consistently followed beats a perfect plan you abandon. Behavior always beats intelligence.

Investing basics to know

📌
Index funds over stock picking. Morgan Housel: "Most investors would be better off buying a total market index fund and never looking at it again."
📌
Time in market beats timing the market. Starting at 25 with $200/month beats starting at 35 with $400/month — compounding is the 8th wonder of the world.
📌
Automate everything. Set it up once. Remove the decision. Behavior is the hardest part — automation solves it.
📌
Save rate > return rate. The rate you save has a far bigger impact on wealth than which fund you pick. Focus there first.
