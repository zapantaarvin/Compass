10 Money Rules

For a Rich Life

The principles your financial plan is built on.

1
Ramit Sethi
Know your dream life before building any budget
Money without purpose becomes a restriction. Define what you're building toward first — your spending plan flows from that vision.
2
Ramit Sethi — Conscious Spending Plan
Automate first, then spend intentionally and guilt-free
Investments and savings leave your account automatically on payday. What remains is yours to spend without guilt or tracking every latte.
3
Ramit Sethi
Keep fixed costs at or below 60% of take-home pay
High fixed costs are the #1 source of financial stress. The lower they are, the more flexibility and freedom you have to invest and enjoy.
4
Morgan Housel — The Psychology of Money
Behavior matters infinitely more than intelligence
Financial success has little to do with IQ. Consistent, boring habits from an average person always beat a genius who acts emotionally.
5
Morgan Housel — The Psychology of Money
Build room for error — survival is the ultimate financial skill
The most important financial plan you can have is never putting yourself in a position where you're forced to sell at a loss. Emergency fund first.
6
Morgan Housel — The Psychology of Money
Wealth is what you don't see — your savings rate is your power
Visible money is spent money. The gap between income and spending, invested consistently, is the engine of all financial freedom.
7
Napoleon Hill — Think and Grow Rich
Definiteness of purpose is the start of all achievement
Without a clear written goal, your efforts scatter. Define exactly what you want, when you want it, and what you're willing to trade for it.
8
Napoleon Hill — Think and Grow Rich
Self-discipline and habits are the engine of every financial win
Knowledge without action is worthless. Build the habit of reviewing money weekly, investing consistently, and saying no to lifestyle inflation.
9
Chinkee Tan — Filipino Financial Coach
Intentional spending always beats emotional spending
Every impulse purchase is a brick removed from your financial foundation. Pause before you buy. Ask: does this move me toward or away from my dream life?
10
All Four Mentors — Universal Principle
Make your plan reasonable, not theoretically perfect
A budget you can maintain for 10 years beats a perfect one that breaks in 30 days. Reasonable and consistent always wins over perfect and abandoned.

Only have a PDF statement? Convert it with AI first.

PDF statements can't be read directly — but you can use a free AI tool to convert them to CSV in under 2 minutes.

1

Download your PDF from your bank

Log into online banking → Statements → Download as PDF

2

Upload it to ChatGPT or Claude

Go to chatgpt.com or claude.ai and start a new chat. Upload your PDF, then paste this prompt:

Please extract all transactions from this bank statement and format them as a CSV with exactly these columns: Date, Description, Amount Rules: - Date format: MM/DD/YYYY - Amount should be positive numbers only (no negatives, no dollar signs) - Exclude credits, deposits, and payments — only include spending/debits - Include every row, even small ones
3

Save the output as a .csv file

Copy the CSV text the AI gives you → open Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac) → paste it → Save As transactions.csv

4

Upload that CSV here

Drop it in the upload zone above. The AI-generated CSV contains no account numbers or sensitive metadata — only dates, descriptions, and amounts.

Expected CSV format

Date,Description,Amount
01/15/2024,WALMART GROCERY,87.43
01/16/2024,NETFLIX,15.99
01/17/2024,SHELL GAS STATION,52.10

The tool auto-detects your column names. Variations like "Posted Date", "Merchant", "Debit", "Charge" all work.
